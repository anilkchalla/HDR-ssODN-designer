CRISPR HDR ssODN Designer

Designs an asymmetric single-stranded oligo donor (ssODN) for homology-directed repair, following Addgene / Richardson et al. 2016: 127 nt total, asymmetric arms (91 nt PAM-proximal + 36 nt PAM-distal), complementary to the non-target strand. Re-cut blocking mutations are suggested, not auto-applied.
